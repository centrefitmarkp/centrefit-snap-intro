How a Snap build runs

From your plans to opening day.

Tap a step to see what happens and what we need from you.

  1. Send us the construction drawings and 3D layout. We mark up every device on the plan and return one quotation covering all disciplines, with the ongoing costs shown up front. You see the full scope online and accept with one tap.
  2. We work directly with your builder on wall penetrations, power, the comms room and the ceiling grid, so nothing is missed and nothing is done twice.
  3. Cabling goes in before the ceilings close: Cat6A data, CCTV, access control, alarm, speakers and TV points. Certified and labelled.
  4. Centrefit is an NBN provider as well as the installer. We order the service, supply the router and have the club online before fit‑off, so there's no third party to chase.
  5. Readers, cameras, alarm panel, keypads, Wi‑Fi, phones, TVs and audio go on and get programmed to the Snap standard. Veyla Protect cameras go in beside the doors.
  6. Every device tested, monitoring live with Back2Base, your team shown the apps, and the as‑built documentation handed over.
  7. The same team answers the phone after you open. Remote support, 24/7 monitoring, and Veyla Protect reporting from day one.
